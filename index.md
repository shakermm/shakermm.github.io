# OneMote — Universal TV Remote

Free universal remote for **9 TV brands**. Controls your TVs entirely over your home WiFi using each brand's own protocol — no accounts, no servers, no subscriptions.

- [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mikeshaker.onemote)

## Supported TVs

Roku · Samsung · LG · Google TV / Chromecast built-in · Fire TV · Apple TV · Sony BRAVIA · Vizio SmartCast · Hisense VIDAA

**[Will it work with my TV?](/compatible)** — pairing, ports, and honest limits for all 9 brands.

## What it does

- Full remote — D-pad, volume, channels, inputs, power, and press-and-hold repeat
- Touchpad mode — true pointer on Samsung and LG, gesture navigation everywhere else
- Apps — launch, favorite, and reorder your TV's apps; shortcuts right on the remote
- Type on your TV — keyboard, paste from clipboard, and live typing on Roku and LG
- What to Watch — search titles across streaming services and open them on the TV
- Cast photos and videos, or mirror your phone's screen
- Voice commands, Siri / Google Assistant shortcuts, and home-screen widgets
- Private listening on Roku — TV audio to your headphones
- Simple mode (big buttons), Kid-safe handoff, and 10 languages
- Turn off every TV in the house with one tap
