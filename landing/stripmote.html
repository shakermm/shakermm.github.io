Colors & effects

Color wheel, warm/cool white, brightness and every built-in effect, with favorites one tap away.

Lights pulse to music using your phone's mic or the strip's own mic. Keeps going while you use other apps.

Group strips into rooms and save scenes like Movie night or Bedtime. Apply them to all lights at once.

Turn lights on, off or to a scene without opening the app. Works with Shortcuts automations too.

Camera color match

Point your camera at anything and the lights match its color. Nothing is saved or uploaded.

Sleep & wake-up light

Fade to off at bedtime, or set a wake-up light that runs on the strip itself, even with your phone away.
