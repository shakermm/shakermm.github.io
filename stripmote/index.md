# Stripmote: LED Strip Lights

Bluetooth remote, scenes and widgets for the LED strip lights you already own. **No subscription. Ever.**

## Get Stripmote

- Download on the App Store — coming soon

## Works with

Bluetooth LED strip controllers that advertise names such as **ELK-BLEDOM, ELK-BLE, ELK-BTC, MELK, LEDBLE, XSL-, LED LIGHT STRIP, BR-01B, BR-0x, DMRRBA** and **L7260**. Infrared-only and Wi-Fi-only strips can't be controlled this way.

Light not recognised? In Stripmote, go to **Add a light → Show all Bluetooth devices**, tap your light and share the report with us — we add new controller families from those reports.

## Help

- **Won't connect?** These controllers accept one connection at a time. Fully close any other LED app (swipe it away), or unplug the strip for 5 seconds, then try again.
- **Red shows as green?** Open the light → **Edit → Fix swapped colors**.
- **Brightness does nothing?** Open the light → **Edit → Brightness → Dim the color**.
- **Bluetooth permission:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth → Stripmote.
