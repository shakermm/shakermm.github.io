# Stripmote Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-11-25
**Live URL:** https://shakermm.github.io/stripmote/privacy

This policy explains what data the Stripmote iOS app ("Stripmote", "the app") collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have.

## 1. Who we are

Stripmote is published by **Mike Shaker, LLC** ("we", "us"), which is the data controller for the processing described here.
Contact: **stripmote@pharaohdigital.co**

## 2. The short version

- **No accounts, no sign-up, and no Stripmote servers.** We do not receive or store your lights, rooms, scenes, settings, audio or images.
- Your lights are controlled **directly from your phone over Bluetooth**.
- The only data that leaves your phone goes to the service providers listed in section 5, for **ads**, the optional **Remove Ads purchase**, **app updates** and **crash reports**.
- **Personalized ads only with your permission.** You can change your ad choices at any time in **Settings → Privacy choices**.

## 3. Data that stays on your phone

Stripmote stores the following **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. We never receive it:

- **Your lights:** the name you give each light, its Bluetooth identifier and advertised name, room, icon, color-order and brightness settings, and last-used color and brightness.
- **Your rooms, scenes, favorite colors and preferences** (haptics, appearance, default light).
- **Widgets, Control Center and Siri/Shortcuts data.** So these features work without opening the app, a copy of your saved lights and scenes is kept in an app-private shared area on your phone (an iOS "App Group") that only Stripmote and its widgets can read.
- **Your Remove Ads status**, cached so the app stays ad-free offline.

**Features that use sensors. Nothing is recorded or transmitted:**

- **Bluetooth:** used to find and control your lights. Commands go straight from your phone to the light.
- **Microphone (phone music sync):** only while you have music sync turned on, Stripmote measures the **loudness** of the sound around you to pulse your lights. Audio is processed in real time in memory and is **never recorded, stored or sent anywhere**.
- **Camera (pick a color):** only while that screen is open. The image is used in memory to read the color you tap and is **never saved or uploaded**.
- **"Show all Bluetooth devices"** (troubleshooting): lists nearby Bluetooth devices on your screen only. A report is shared **only if you tap Share** and choose where to send it. It contains just that device's Bluetooth name, signal strength and advertised identifiers.

Stripmote does **not** request your location, contacts, photos, health data or any account information.

## 6. Advertising

- Stripmote shows **one small banner** at the bottom of its main screens. There are no pop-up, full-screen, video or rewarded ads, no ads during setup, and no ads in widgets.
- Before any ad loads, Google's **User Messaging Platform** asks for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws). Apple's **App Tracking Transparency** prompt asks whether ads may use your advertising identifier.
- If you decline tracking, ads are **non-personalized**. Where consent is legally required and not given, **no ads are shown at all**.
- Ad content is limited to **PG** ratings.
- Buying **Remove Ads** (one-time, never a subscription) removes the banner for good, and the app stops loading the ad SDK.

## 7. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

- **Consent:** personalized advertising, and storing or accessing information on your device for ads. You can withdraw consent at any time in **Settings → Privacy choices**.
- **Performance of a contract:** processing your *Remove Ads* purchase and restoring it.
- **Legitimate interests:** crash reporting to keep the app working, delivering app updates and security fixes, and non-personalized ads where permitted. You may object at any time (see section 10).

## 8. Retention

- **On your phone:** kept until you delete it (remove a light, or uninstall the app, which deletes everything).
- **RevenueCat:** purchase records are kept while you have the purchase, and as required for tax and accounting.
- **Sentry:** crash reports are deleted automatically, normally within 90 days.
- **Google AdMob** and **Expo:** as described in their policies linked above.

## 9. US state privacy rights (including California)

Showing **personalized** ads with your advertising identifier may count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising under some US state laws. You can **opt out** at any time by:

- choosing **Settings → Privacy choices** in Stripmote, and/or
- turning off **Allow Apps to Request to Track** in iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

You also have the right to know about, access, correct and delete your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Email us at the address in section 1. We hold no account data, so we may only be able to act on data held by our service providers, and we will help you do that.

## 10. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can:

- **Change or withdraw ad consent:** Settings → Privacy choices.
- **Stop tracking / reset your advertising identifier:** iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Turn off Bluetooth, microphone or camera access** for Stripmote in iOS Settings at any time.
- **Delete your data:** remove lights in the app, or uninstall Stripmote to delete everything on your phone. To ask us to delete your *Remove Ads* purchase record held by RevenueCat, email us (you may need to restore the purchase afterwards).
- **Access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction and portability** (EEA/UK) and **complaints** to your local data-protection authority.

We respond to requests within 30 days (45 days where US state law allows).

## 11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

## 12. Security

Data on your phone is protected by iOS app sandboxing and, when your phone is locked, by iOS data protection. Data sent to service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we limit what is collected to the minimum described above.

## 13. Children

Stripmote is rated 4+ on the App Store but is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires a higher age). Stripmote itself collects no personal information from anyone. Ad requests are limited by a maximum content rating and are not treated as child-directed; if you believe a child's data was collected through ads, contact us and we will help with deletion.

## 14. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Effective date" above shows the latest version. Material changes will be highlighted in the app or re-prompted through the consent flow where required.
