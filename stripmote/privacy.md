# Stripmote Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-11-25
**Live URL:** https://shakermm.github.io/stripmote/privacy

Stripmote ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Mike Shaker, LLC and controls Bluetooth LED strip lights from your iPhone. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

## What Stripmote does

- **Finds and controls your lights over Bluetooth.** Commands (power, color, brightness, effects, scenes) go **directly from your phone to the light's controller**. There are no Stripmote servers and no accounts.
- **Remembers your lights on your phone only:** the name you give each light, its Bluetooth identifier and advertised name, room, icon, color-order setting, last-used color/brightness, favorite colors and scenes.
- **Widgets, Control Center and Siri/Shortcuts:** so these can work without opening the app, a small copy of your saved lights and scenes is kept in an app-private shared storage area on your phone (an iOS "App Group") that only Stripmote and its widgets can read. It never leaves your phone.
- **Music sync (phone microphone):** when you turn on phone-mic music sync, Stripmote measures the **loudness** of the sound around you to pulse your lights. Audio is analysed on your phone in real time, is **never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere**, and the microphone is used only while music sync is on. Stripmote asks for microphone permission only when you first open this feature.
- **Pick a color from the camera:** the camera view is used on your phone to read the color you tap. Images are **never saved or uploaded**. Stripmote asks for camera permission only when you first open this feature.
- **"Show all Bluetooth devices"** (troubleshooting) lists nearby Bluetooth devices on your screen. Nothing is sent anywhere unless **you** tap *Share* and choose where to send the report (it contains only that device's Bluetooth name, signal strength and advertised identifiers).

## Data we do NOT collect

- **No accounts, no sign-up, no Stripmote servers.**
- **No location.** Stripmote never asks for your location. (iOS shows Bluetooth as a separate permission; we only use it to talk to your lights.)
- **Your lights, rooms, scenes and settings stay on your phone.**
- **No audio or images** are recorded, stored or transmitted.

## Third-party services

Stripmote uses a few services to show ads, sell the optional Remove Ads purchase, deliver app updates and report crashes. Each receives only what it needs:

| Service | What it does | Data it receives |
|---|---|---|
| **Google AdMob** (with Google's User Messaging Platform) | One small banner ad | Advertising identifier (only with your permission), approximate location from IP, device and ad-interaction data. [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) |
| **RevenueCat** | Processes the one-time *Remove Ads* purchase and *Restore purchases* | An anonymous app user ID, your App Store purchase receipt, app version, device model and OS. No name or email. [RevenueCat privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) |
| **Apple App Store** | Payment for *Remove Ads* | Handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. |
| **Expo (EAS Update)** | Delivers app updates | IP address, app and runtime version, platform. [Expo privacy policy](https://expo.dev/privacy) |
| **Sentry** | Crash reports, so we can fix bugs | Crash stack traces, app version, device model and OS version. **No personal data**; IP addresses are not stored. [Sentry privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/) |

## Advertising

- Stripmote shows **one small banner** at the bottom of the main screens. No pop-up, full-screen, video or rewarded ads, no ads during setup, and no ads in widgets.
- We use Google's **User Messaging Platform** to ask for consent where required (for example, under GDPR in the European Economic Area and UK, and applicable US state laws) and Apple's **App Tracking Transparency** prompt.
- If you decline tracking, ads are **non-personalized**. Where consent is legally required and not given, **no ads are shown**.
- Ads are limited to content rated **PG**.
- **Remove Ads** (a one-time purchase, never a subscription) removes the banner permanently; the app then stops loading the ad SDK.

## Permissions used

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Bluetooth | Find and control your lights |
| Microphone | Phone music sync only, when you turn it on — analysed on your phone, never recorded |
| Camera | Pick a color from the camera, only when you open it — never saved or uploaded |
| Tracking (iOS ATT) | Personalized ads, only with your permission |

## Data storage & security

- All app data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone (and the app-private App Group used by widgets).
- Uninstalling Stripmote deletes all of it.

## Children's privacy

Stripmote is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Ad requests are limited to PG-rated content and are not child-directed.

## Your choices

- Re-open ad consent at any time in **Settings → Privacy choices**.
- Limit tracking or reset your advertising identifier in iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- Turn off Bluetooth, microphone or camera access for Stripmote in iOS Settings at any time.
- Remove a light (Light → Edit → Remove light) or uninstall the app to delete everything.
